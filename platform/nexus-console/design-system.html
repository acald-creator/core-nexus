Colour

Roles, not hues. Grounds step up a lightness ladder (dark: 0.14 → 0.30; light: 0.935 → 0.995), inks sit at the far end, and the accent is the Nexus violet at the lightness that carries text. Every text/ground pair in pairs.json passes WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes.

Status & severity

Colour is never the only signal. Health dots differ in shape; severities and phases are printed as words. In the light theme all status inks must sit below L 0.56 to pass as text, which compresses their lightness ladder, so the second cue is doing real work there.
